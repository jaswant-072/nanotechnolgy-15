Nanotechnology in Healthcare

Nanomedicine

Targeted Drug Delivery

Lipid and polymeric nanoparticles transport chemotherapy drugs directly to tumor cells, minimizing damage to healthy tissue and reducing side effects.

Diagnostics

Quantum Dot Biosensors

Semiconductor nanocrystals emit bright, size-tunable fluorescence to detect cancer biomarkers and viral pathogens at ultra-low concentrations.

Regenerative Medicine

Nanofiber Tissue Scaffolds

Biocompatible electrospun nanofibers mimic the extracellular matrix, accelerating bone, cartilage, and neural tissue regeneration.

Nanotechnology in Energy

Solar Power

Perovskite & Quantum Dot Solar Cells

Nanostructured light-absorbing layers capture a broader spectrum of sunlight, boosting photovoltaic conversion efficiency while lowering manufacturing costs.

Energy Storage

Silicon-Nanowire Li-Ion Batteries

Nanostructured anodes increase electrode surface area, enabling faster charging speeds and significantly higher energy density for electric vehicles.

Clean Fuel

Nanocatalysts for Green Hydrogen

Platinum-group and transition-metal nanoparticles accelerate water-splitting electrolysis, making clean hydrogen fuel production more viable.
